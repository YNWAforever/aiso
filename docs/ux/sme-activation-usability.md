# SME sample trial

**Status: human usability pending.** The tasks below are a trial script, not observed results. Four of five actual SME participants completing independently is a proposed target only.

Open the generated English or Hong Kong Chinese sample HTML in a browser. It uses fictitious Harbour Tea data and keeps changes in the current tab. The sample actor selector changes the demonstration view; it does not sign anyone in or grant access. Reset returns to the initial draft. Package downloads, provider actions and other application destinations are outside this sample.

Give one task at a time. Ask the participant to describe what they expect and what they see. Record assistance and exact behavior; give no button-location or technical hints during an independent attempt.

| Task | 香港中文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 你之前儲存咗一項工作。由今日首頁搵返佢，講出下一步。 | Find your previously saved work from Today and explain its next step. |
| 2 | 查看內容同儲存證據，準備並提交審批。主測試可以直接用原有文字；如要修改，請用示例指引列出嘅確切替代內容。 | Inspect the content and saved evidence, then prepare and submit it for review. Use the supplied text, or the exact alternative shown in sample instructions. |
| 3 | 以工作擁有人身份，解釋點解目前未能完成審批。畫面有乜資料？仲有乜未能確定？ | As the submitter, explain the review blocker, what information is available, and what remains unknown. |
| 4 | 切換到示例獨立審批人。假設呢份示例內容已符合要求，主流程請批核並填原因；返回工作擁有人，確認結果。 | Switch to the sample independent reviewer. Assume the supplied content meets requirements; approve it with a reason for this main scenario, then return to the owner and confirm it. |
| 5 | 假設你已自行完成交付，選擇手動途徑，填示例目的地、指引提供嘅 UTC 時間同備註。 | Assume you delivered the work yourself. Choose the manual route and enter a sample destination, the supplied UTC time, and a note. |
| 6 | 解釋畫面記錄咗乜：有冇核實網站已修改？有冇足夠觀察資料話成效改善？ | Explain what was recorded: was a website change verified, and is there enough observation evidence to claim improvement? |
| 7 | 觀察者設定一次讀取失敗或遺失交付回覆後，繼續處理，留意輸入同記錄有冇保留。 | After the observer arms one read failure or lost delivery reply, recover and check whether input and records were preserved. |

The sample suggests **2026-11-14 00:10:00 UTC**, inside its explicit sample timeline. An immediate delivery record has no later observation: outcome windows remain not due. It does not create a seven-day result, verify publication, collect new evidence or demonstrate causal business improvement.
