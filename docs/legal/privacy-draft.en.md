## What we collect and why

When you create or use an account, we process account identifiers, your email address, profile information, authentication/session data and your organisation or client records to provide access and administer the service. Required fields are identified where information is collected; without information needed for authentication or a requested feature, we may be unable to provide it.

When you request a scan, we process the submitted website address, selected settings and information obtained from the public website or supplied by you. We use these inputs to produce and retain checks, scores, diagnostic information and reports. Website content may itself contain personal data. Do not submit private URLs, credentials or personal data that are unnecessary for the task, and ensure you are entitled to provide information about clients or other people.

Monitoring and content-generation features may process selected questions, brand information, sampled answers and proposed content. These outputs may contain personal data or inaccurate statements and require human review. They are not a complete record of everything an AI platform says.

Payment and subscription administration involves account and subscription identifiers and payment-status information. Payment providers handle payment details through their own services. We also process service and security information needed to handle requests, control abuse, investigate errors and maintain the service. Correspondence that you send us is used to respond and manage the issue.

A collection notice at the relevant form or interaction will explain its specific purposes, whether provision is obligatory or voluntary, the consequences of not providing required information, possible recipient classes, and the contact for access or correction. This policy does not replace that notice.

## Scan evidence and public access

A stored scan can include normalised check outputs and diagnostic snapshots. The proposed version 1 evidence attachment records bounded parsed signals and collection limitations; its URL descriptors retain origins with redaction flags rather than paths, query strings or fragments. It does not store raw page excerpts. These limits apply to that attachment, not automatically to every legacy check detail, monitoring answer or other service record.

A result link may expose a limited summary, including the scanned domain and score, without requiring account ownership. Full account results are restricted through the service's access controls. Where a report-sharing feature is used, recipients may be able to view the information included in the shared report. Consider the intended audience before sharing; recipients may retain copies outside our control.

## Providers and other recipients

We use service providers for hosting, database storage, authentication, payment processing, email delivery and AI processing. Relevant inputs may be sent to AI providers to perform the feature you request. Configured workflows or webhooks may also receive scan information. The provider inventory and applicable processing locations must be identified in **[APPROVED PROVIDER AND LOCATION NOTICE]**.

We will limit disclosures to what is needed for the relevant purpose and apply appropriate contractual or other safeguards. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to address a lawful request or protect legal rights. A service provider may process data outside Hong Kong; do not assume that all data stays in Hong Kong. Links to third-party sites are governed by those sites' own practices.

## Cookies and similar storage

Authentication and session mechanisms use cookies or similar storage to maintain access and protect account flows. **[APPROVED COOKIE NOTICE]** will identify the cookies or storage used, their purposes and duration, and any optional tools and choices. Restricting necessary cookies may prevent account features from working. We will explain and obtain any required consent before introducing optional tracking or using personal data for direct marketing.

## Retention and protection

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes for which it is held, including applicable legal and dispute-handling requirements. Retention periods and disposal procedures are described in **[APPROVED RETENTION SCHEDULE]**. Scan evidence currently follows the lifecycle of its scan record; no automatic deletion period is promised here. Account closure does not itself establish when every scan, shared copy, log, backup or provider record is erased.

We use access controls and technical safeguards to protect information, and review access and processing arrangements. No system can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your account credentials and report suspected misuse through **[SECURITY CONTACT]**. Do not upload secrets or sensitive material unnecessarily.

## Access, correction and questions

You may ask whether we hold your personal data and request access or correction in accordance with applicable law. Send requests to the privacy contact above with enough information for us to identify the relevant records. We may need proportionate identity verification to protect your information. We will handle requests within applicable statutory requirements and explain any permitted refusal or applicable fee. You may also ask about retention or disposal of your information; we will explain relevant legal or operational constraints.

If a concern remains unresolved, you may contact Hong Kong's Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data through its official website.

## Updates

We will publish material changes with an updated effective date and give additional notice where appropriate. A new purpose of use will be handled in accordance with applicable consent and notice requirements.
