## Delivered boundary

C9b adds an authenticated bilingual observation workspace, `GET /api/clients/[clientId]/observations`, fixed DTO/query/projection contracts and one account-scoped snapshot read over retained Pulse rows and current monitored questions. The implemented production paths are:

- `lib/observations/{types,query,schema,store,service}.ts`
- `app/api/clients/[clientId]/observations/route.ts`
- `app/[lang]/dashboard/[clientId]/observations/page.tsx`

- `components/dashboard/DashboardSidebar.tsx`
- `messages/en.json`, `messages/zh-HK.json`
- `.github/workflows/pr-gate.yml` and `scripts/ci/prepare-component-fixtures.mjs`

Focused tests are in `__tests__/observations`, `__tests__/api/observations.test.ts`, `__tests__/components/observation-page.test.tsx`, `__tests__/components/observation-render.test.tsx`, `__tests__/ci/component-fixtures.test.ts` and `tests/e2e/c9b-observations.spec.ts`.

The exact response and read semantics are recorded in `docs/contracts/fields.md`; route parameters, guards and errors are recorded in `docs/contracts/routes.md`. Counts describe stored retained rows before status filtering and pagination. They are not a provider-attempt denominator. Exact legacy collection time, model and market remain unknown. Raw answers never enter the DTO. Current prompt metadata never replaces the stored historical question.

## C9c consumption boundary

C9c may consume `ObservationResponse`, `Question`, `Observation`, `ObservationQuery`, `parseObservationQuery`, `projectObservation`, `loadObservationSnapshot` and `loadAuthenticatedObservations` by their named exports. It must preserve account-scoped ownership, the successful-row predicate, null provenance, fixed projections, bounded menus/pages, one-snapshot count consistency and the existing Pulse KPI logic. C9b does not authorize C9c persistence, automatic saving, provider execution or a new public route; those remain governed by the separately approved C9c design and execution scope.
